Bonus attachments — Module C v2

SVG · 960 × 2191 mm sheet · 1 unit = 1 mm

Four attachments built around one shared part — the traverse-rate unit, a slotted friction-drive that gives the spiral's growth rate and the two helices' pitch genuinely stepless, continuously-adjustable control (the same slot-and-clamp principle as the trammel's beam), in place of a fixed-diameter post. Helix and spiralling helix are new in v2 — they never had a physical design before.
