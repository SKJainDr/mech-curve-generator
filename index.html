Universal Conic & Curve Generator

One physical instrument, four modules and a bonus attachment set, that draws any conic section by eccentricity — circle, ellipse, parabola, hyperbola, and a pair of crossing lines — plus six more curves for free: sine wave, cycloid, cardioid, spiral, helix, and spiralling helix.

One dial, five conics

Every conic section is the same family of curves, distinguished by a single number: eccentricity. This instrument builds that idea into hardware. A sliding beam on two perpendicular tracks (the same trick behind the classic “circles draw a straight line” toy) sweeps out a circle or an ellipse depending on where you clamp the pen. A focus pin and a directrix rail, tied together with a taut thread, trace a true parabola or hyperbola. Two rulers hinged at a fixed angle give you the fifth conic — a pair of straight lines — at whatever crossing angle you like. Turn the same eccentricity dial past e = 1 and the hyperbola opens out into that angle automatically, via e = sec(α/2).

Build it yourself

Every module is cut from 6mm ply/MDF and standard M6 hardware — no custom machining beyond a scroll saw or laser cutter. The blueprints are true to scale (1 SVG unit = 1mm), dimensioned, and include a full bill of materials, so you can print them as cutting templates or open them directly in Inkscape/Illustrator.
